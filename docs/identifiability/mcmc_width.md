# Why `refine.mcmc` intervals disagree with the profile likelihood (R2c)

In R1c the 68 % interval of d from `refine.mcmc` agreed with the Poisson profile likelihood within a
factor 2 for one case of three: it was 4.7 times wider for `mid_no_oxide` and 2–3 times narrower for
`t2_low`. Candidate causes: the likelihood (Gaussian in log₁₀ R with one fitted spread, while the
counts are Poisson), the sampler (2000 steps, half discarded, no convergence check), or the
comparison itself (profile on noise-free data from `fim.py`'s model, MCMC on one noisy curve under
refine's model, which differs from it at the grid edges).

## Pre-registration (written before running)

Registered 2026-10-23, before any of the computations below were run. Changes go in a new commit
that says why.

**Data and comparator.** To take the third cause out, everything here uses one model and one
curve per case: the R2b curves (`refine_causes.py`: Poisson draw, seed 0, 10⁶ counts, generated by
refine's own `make_forward` at the truth) and refine's forward model for every likelihood. The
comparator is the profile likelihood of d **on that same noisy curve** under refine's model: at
fixed d, the Poisson deviance minimized over the other free parameters (bounded least squares, as
the R2b reference fit), Δχ²(d) = D(d) − D(ML). Its Δχ² = 1 crossings on each side are found by
stepping out from the ML d and bisecting; the profile half-width h_p is the mean of the two
distances. Bounds for everything: the global bounds of `nonunique.BOUNDS`, as in R1c's MCMC runs.

**Arms** (all started from the ML fit of the reference least squares, all with flat priors within
the same bounds, emcee with refine.mcmc's walker count and start ball, first half discarded):

| arm | likelihood | length |
|---|---|---|
| G | `refine.mcmc.sample_posterior` as it is: Gaussian in log₁₀ R over nonzero counts, one fitted spread | 2000 steps (R1c) |
| G-long | the same | until converged (below) |
| P | Poisson, log L = Σ (y ln λ − λ) over all points, zero counts included, λ = 10⁶ R | until converged |

*Converged*: the kept half of the chain is at least 50 times the largest integrated autocorrelation
time over all parameters (emcee's estimate). Lengths tried: 2000, 8000, 20000 steps; an arm still
short of 50 τ at 20000 is reported as unconverged, with its τ.

**Width.** h_m = (p84 − p16) / 2 of d. Agreement: 0.5 ≤ h_m / h_p ≤ 2.

**Decision rules.**
- If G already agrees in all three cases on these curves, R1c's disagreement came from the
  comparison (noise-free profile under a different model), and neither the likelihood nor the
  sampler is blamed.
- Otherwise: if P agrees in all three cases, the cause is the likelihood ("likelihood problem"),
  provided G-long still disagrees in at least one; if G-long agrees in all three, the cause is chain
  length ("sampler problem").
- If P is converged and still disagrees in some case, neither explanation holds for that case and it
  is reported as open.
- An unconverged arm gives no verdict for its case.

**Cost.** refine's forward model for 52 walkers takes a few milliseconds, so 20000 steps are about
2 minutes per arm and case; the profile about a minute per case.

### Amendments (with reasons)

1. *Profile inner fits restarted (2026-10-23, after the physics review of PR #24, before any
   verdict).* A profile point continued from its neighbour with one local fit only bounds PL from
   above, and R1c's t2_low showed that the nuisances have more than one basin (two ML solutions that
   differ only in the Kα2 ratio). Each fixed-d point is now the lowest of three local fits: continued,
   nuisances at refine's initial values, nuisances at their bound midpoints. The comparator profile
   had already been computed once without restarts (h_p 0.160 Å for mid_no_oxide); it is recomputed
   with restarts and only the recomputed one is used. The MCMC arms are unchanged.
2. *ML re-anchored (2026-10-23, after the arms had run).* With restarts, some profile points fell
   below the least-squares "ML" by up to 0.70 (t2_low) and 0.45 (mid_oxide): that fit from the truth
   had stopped in a nuisance basin above the minimum. Δχ² must be measured from the minimum, so when
   a profile point is more than 0.05 below the ML, every parameter is refitted from that point and
   the profile is recomputed (one round each for mid_oxide and t2_low). The ML thickness moved from
   239.87 to 239.50 Å (mid_oxide) and from 198.04 to 196.65 Å (t2_low). The MCMC arms had started
   from the old ML. A start point only shifts the burn-in, but none of the chains converged (below),
   so this cannot be checked from these runs.

## Results

Reproduce (about 45 minutes on CPU, mostly the 20000-step chains; needs `uv sync --extra mcmc` and
the R2b curves, which `refine_causes.py` generates on the fly):

```bash
uv run python experiments/001_identifiability/mcmc_width.py profile G G-long P report
```

Table: [`mcmc_width_tables.md`](mcmc_width_tables.md); data: `mcmc_width.json`.

| case | h_p (Å) | G: h_m, ratio | G-long (20000): h_m, ratio, τ | P (20000): h_m, ratio, τ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| mid_oxide | 1.016 | 0.679, 0.67 | 0.748, 0.74, 291 | 0.652, 0.64, 357 |
| mid_no_oxide | 0.160 | 0.367, 2.29 | 0.399, 2.49, 208 | 0.086, 0.54, 231 |
| t2_low | 1.266 | 1.037, 0.82 | 1.155, 0.91, 653 | 1.999, 1.58, 893 |

**Verdict by the registered rules: none.** No arm converged. At 20000 steps the kept half is 10000
steps, against 50 τ = 10400 to 44700, so every arm is unconverged and gives no verdict for its case.
The chains of `refine.mcmc` as shipped (2000 steps, 1000 kept) are 4 to 5 times shorter than their
own 50 τ. R1c's MCMC intervals came from such chains.

What can be read without a verdict (inference, not established):

- The half-widths change little between 2000, 8000 and 20000 steps for the Gaussian likelihood
  (0.68 → 0.73 → 0.75 Å for mid_oxide). The Poisson chain of t2_low does not settle (1.27 → 2.17 →
  2.00 Å).
- With these unconverged chains, the Poisson-likelihood widths are within the factor 2 in all three
  cases (0.54–1.58). The Gaussian log₁₀ R likelihood is outside it for mid_no_oxide (2.3–2.5 times
  wider) and inside for the other two. That is what a likelihood problem would look like, but the
  registered rule needs converged chains, so it stays a hypothesis.
- R1c's "4.7 times wider for mid_no_oxide" becomes 2.3–2.5 times here, with a profile whose crossing
  is found by bisection on a single curve and model. The rest of the R1c factor came from the
  comparison: the profile grid, the noise-free curve and the different model.

**Next.** Converging these chains needs either about 100000 steps per arm and case (about 6 hours of
CPU for the three cases and two long arms; [HEAVY]) or a sampler that mixes better in these
correlated 12–13-dimensional posteriors, for example emcee's differential-evolution moves or
stretch moves in coordinates whitened by the Fisher matrix at the ML. Either one is a change to the
registered lengths and goes in as a new amendment before it is run. Separately, `refine.mcmc`
should report τ and warn when the chain is shorter than 50 τ. It currently discards half the chain
and reports the rest without a check.

### Amendment 3: whitened differential-evolution chains (registered 2026-10-23, before running)

Chosen by the coordinator over 100000-step chains. Nothing below has been run when this is written.

- **Arms.** G-DE: the Gaussian log₁₀ R likelihood with one fitted spread, exactly as
  `refine.mcmc.sample_posterior` computes it (same residuals, same lnσ bounds and term, weight 1).
  P-DE: the Poisson likelihood of arm P. Same model, curve, bounds and flat priors as before.
- **Start.** The re-anchored ML of amendment 2, with lnσ at the log of the RMS log₁₀ residual
  there (as `refine.mcmc` starts it).
- **Coordinates.** θ = θ_ML + L z, with L the Cholesky factor of F⁻¹, F the Poisson Fisher
  information at the ML under refine's model (central-difference Jacobian of ln λ). The transform is
  linear, so flat priors in θ stay flat in z; bounds are checked in θ. lnσ (G-DE only) is appended
  unscaled. The same whitening serves both likelihoods; it is exact only for P-DE.
- **Sampler.** emcee with 0.8 `DEMove` + 0.2 `DESnookerMove`, 64 walkers, started in a ball of radius
  0.1 in z (clipped into the bounds), seed 0.
- **Length and convergence.** 5000 steps; if the kept half (2500 steps) is shorter than 50 τ, one
  more run of 15000 steps. τ is emcee's integrated autocorrelation time of each θ coordinate over the
  kept half, maximum over parameters, as before. An arm still short of 50 τ at 15000 steps is
  unconverged and gives no verdict.
- **Width and rules.** Unchanged: h_m from the 16–84 % range of d, comparator h_p from the
  re-anchored profile, agreement 0.5–2, and the decision rules above, with G-DE in place of G-long
  and P-DE in place of P. G (refine.mcmc as shipped, 2000 steps) keeps its recorded result.

## Results of amendment 3

Reproduce (about 50 minutes on CPU, with other jobs running):
`uv run python experiments/001_identifiability/mcmc_width.py profile G-DE P-DE report`.

| case | h_p (Å) | G-DE: h_m, ratio, τ at 15000 | P-DE: h_m, ratio, τ at 15000 | converged (50 τ ≤ 7500) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| mid_oxide | 1.016 | 0.758, 0.75, 85 | 0.635, 0.62, 104 | both |
| mid_no_oxide | 0.160 | 0.392, **2.45**, 90 | 0.083, 0.52, 57 | both |
| t2_low | 1.266 | 1.238, 0.98, 345 | 2.076, 1.64, 603 | neither |

Whitening and DE moves cut τ by a factor of 2 to 4 against the stretch move at the same length
(mid_oxide 85 vs 291, mid_no_oxide 90 vs 208), enough for two of the three cases to converge within
15000 steps.

**Verdict by the registered rules, per case:**
- mid_oxide: both likelihoods agree with the profile (0.75, 0.62). No disagreement to explain.
- mid_no_oxide: the converged Gaussian log₁₀ R chain is still 2.45 times wider than the profile, and
  the converged Poisson chain agrees (0.52). For this case the cause is the **likelihood**, not the
  chain length: refine.mcmc's chain was short (#29), but running it to convergence leaves the
  width where it was (0.37 → 0.39 Å).
- t2_low: no verdict. Neither chain converges. τ grows with chain length (P-DE 219 → 603), which is
  what a sampler crossing between separate basins looks like. R1c had already found two ML solutions
  of this curve that differ only in the Kα2 ratio (0.51 vs 0.00) within ΔD = 1.

The registered all-case rule ("P agrees in all three cases") cannot be applied, because t2_low gives
no verdict. What is established: of the two R1c disagreements, the mid_no_oxide one is a likelihood
problem. The t2_low one ("2–3 times narrower") came from unconverged chains and a coarse comparison;
with the profile re-anchored, the unconverged widths are 0.8–1.6 times the profile.

Not part of the rules:
- P-DE for mid_no_oxide sits at the edge of the agreement band (0.52). Its 68 % interval (239.88–240.04
  Å) is narrower than the profile's Δχ² ≤ 1 interval (239.79–240.11 Å), which is asymmetric (0.202 Å
  below the ML, 0.118 Å above). Profile and marginal can differ when a nuisance sits at a bound (here the Kα2
  ratio), because the marginal integrates over volume that the bound cuts off. Not tested here.
- t2_low is the first case in this experiment where the posterior itself, not only the optimizer,
  shows more than one nuisance basin. It is a candidate for the R2a question, on the nuisance side.
